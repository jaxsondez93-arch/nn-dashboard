Correlation matrix (Pearson r)

How strongly do two metrics move together during this period? Blue = they rise together, red = one rises as the other falls. Dim cells are not statistically significant. Click a cell to plot it.

Rolling correlation

Pearson r over a sliding window for the selected pair: does the relationship hold steady, or come and go?

What explains the variation? (multiple regression, R²)

Pick a metric and the others to explain it with. R² says what share of its ups and downs they account for; the out-of-sample figure says whether that would hold on time periods the fit has not seen.

Training loss

Per-step loss (averaged per bucket). Includes an irreducible ranking-entropy floor, so flat ≠ proven stalled.

Validation regret (lower is better)

Regret at a 30-aim budget on the 55-board validation set (4 games). Noisy: roughly ±0.12 between evals. Evaluated every 15 min.

Training speed

Steps per minute between heartbeats (drops mean throttling or load).

CPU temperature

Heartbeat temperature with hot and critical lines. Breaks in the line = the machine was off or suspended.

Battery

Charge level over time. A falling line with no training activity means the machine was suspended on battery.

Memory available

MB available (from the 5-minute sampler, if it is running).

Machine stops

Gaps of more than 3 minutes in the heartbeat: the machine was off, suspended, or the trainer was stopped. Shown for the selected period.
