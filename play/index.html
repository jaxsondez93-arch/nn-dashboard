The bot

Speed runs more of the same fixed game frames each screen refresh, drawing only the last one; none are skipped. The bot's thinking time is not affected.

The strategy is fixed for the length of a game; a change takes effect when the next game starts.

Press Start. The first game begins on its own.
Loading the best bot...

Best bot: a learned scorer ranks all 781 aims and the game's own physics simulates the most promising ones. It then chooses, in order: survive, break blocks that are about to become last-chance, get the extra ball, do the most damage. If every tested shot would lose, it searches the rest until one survives. It tries fewer shots when nothing is near the line. It thinks on your device; the other strategies use no simulator.

Build a puzzle

The game builds the board you describe, then the bot can play on from it. One number is enough: leave a box empty and it is filled in the way a real game has it at that point (blocks, balls, health and score all grow together). What you type is never changed. Fill in more than one and the empty ones follow the average of what your numbers point to. Numbers typed in the boxes always win over the scene box below, and the boxes empty once their numbers are in the scene.

Or write the whole scene yourself. Each block is [x, y, health]: x runs 60 (left) to 660 (right), y runs 150 (near the top, the danger end: a block this high must break at once) to 1270 (the bottom, where new rows appear), blocks at least 100 apart. The bot must be paused first.

On a phone? The light puzzle page has just these controls and the game, so it is smoother. The puzzle editor lets you place every block and the pickup, set every number and fire test shots.
