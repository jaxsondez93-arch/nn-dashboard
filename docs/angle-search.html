Does splitting the angle in half find the best shot?

An earlier version of this page is retracted. It reconstructed a tap position from an angle and a radius, on the assumption that only direction matters to the physics. That assumption was wrong: the same nominal angle, at different radii, reported wildly different damage on the same board — the shot physics is sensitive to launch angle far more finely than degrees, and reconstructing a tap this way doesn't reliably reproduce one. The chart and the "27° vs 50.8°" numbers from that version are gone, along with the claim that the bisection shots came from real stored pixels — they didn't; they went through the same broken reconstruction.

Redone below using only real, already-computed tap positions from the project's own pixels781.json — no angle is ever reconstructed, so every shot here is one the real game would actually receive, and results are fully deterministic (repeating a shot gives the same damage every time; checked directly).
