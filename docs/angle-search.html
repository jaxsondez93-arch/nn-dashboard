Does splitting the angle in half find the best shot? (retracted)

This page's chart and numbers are withdrawn. They were built by reconstructing a tap pixel from an angle and a radius, on the assumption that only the direction from the launch point matters to the physics. Checking that assumption afterward: the same nominal angle, at different radii, reported wildly different damage (9, 14, 16, 17, 11, 15, 20, 9, 13 of 15, all for the identical angle on the identical board) — the game's shot physics is sensitive to the exact launch angle at a precision far finer than degrees, and reconstructing a pixel this way doesn't reliably reproduce a given angle. The real conclusion that stands: a two-shot bisection search misses the best aim on most boards, because this project only ever tests real pixels from pixels781.json, never angle reconstructions, so that number is unaffected. Everything about why it misses, and any angle-indexed chart, is not trustworthy and has been taken down pending a corrected method.
