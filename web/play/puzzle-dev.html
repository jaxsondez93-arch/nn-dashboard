    What everything means
    • Blocks are 120 by 120. They rise one row (126) each turn, and a block that reaches the loss line ends the game. Two things must be at least 100 apart; the board is 720 wide and 1280 tall, and a block's centre must be inside x 60 to 660, y 150 to 1270.
    • The pickup adds one ball when a ball touches it. The game only ever has one: it makes the next only when the pickup timer reads 7, and the timer is already past 7 while one is on the board. So with the pickup placed the timer is set for you (8 plus the turns it has risen).
    • Balls are fired one every 0.3 s, all along the aim. Health is hit points: every hit takes 1 off. The game keeps these as 64-bit floating-point numbers: whole numbers are exact up to 9,007,199,254,740,991; above 2^53 a hit may take off 0 or 2, and from 2^54 a block can never be destroyed.
    • New-block health is the health the next row's blocks get; it goes up by 1 every 3 turns, and the health counter says how far along that is. The turn is only used to fill the empty numbers in as a real game at that turn would have them.
    • The aim is a direction from the dot at the top. The test shot taps the game at that aim and waits for the whole turn; then Read the board from the game brings the new board (one row higher, with a new random row) back here.
