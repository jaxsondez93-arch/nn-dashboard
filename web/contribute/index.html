The details

  • Stop any time: untick the box or close the page. Unticking stops the work, and results already finished are still sent while the page stays open. Anything unsent is saved on your device and sent the next time you open this page. The work always pauses when the page is hidden. How hard it works is up to you (the slider, 25% to begin with), and the safety switches are on until you turn one off.
  • What is sent: a task number and, for each practice shot, three numbers (damage done, how close it came to losing and extra balls collected), compressed to save messages. No name, account, device details or location. The results travel to the project owner through ntfy.sh, a free public message relay, so ntfy.sh sees your internet address, as any website does. The channel is not secret: someone who finds its name could read these numbers or post to it, which is one reason every result is re-checked.
  • Results are checked, not trusted: the owner re-computes a sample of them. As with any web page, a technically skilled person can inspect what their own browser does; altered results are detected and thrown away.
  • No install, no permissions, no camera, microphone, location or files. Full explanation.
